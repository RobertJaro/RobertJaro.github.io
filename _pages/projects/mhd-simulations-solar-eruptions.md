**Connect the magnetic energy stored before an eruption with the plasma that moves when it is released.** Magnetohydrodynamics describes the coupled evolution of magnetic fields and conducting plasma, providing the physical setting for this research direction.

## Toward a coupled model of the solar atmosphere

The [Digital Twin of the Sun initiative at NSF NCAR](https://ncarprojects.ucar.edu/ai/science/sun) brings together our magnetic-field reconstructions and coronal-plasma tomography. The aim is to combine complementary observations within a coherent three-dimensional physical model and follow the interactions that shape solar eruptions.

The existing foundations are [NF2 magnetic reconstructions](/projects/force-free-extrapolations/), [EUV tomography](/projects/euv-tomography/), and [white-light CME tomography](/projects/white-light-tomography/). **Coupling these capabilities is ongoing research.** The published reconstruction studies provide observational constraints and magnetic diagnostics. The next step is to test their integration in coupled dynamical models.

## From eruption to space weather

Our collaborative review **CME propagation through the heliosphere** examines how coronal mass ejections interact with the surrounding solar wind. A central conclusion is that uncertain initial conditions and the evolving background environment limit forecasts. Better observations, coordinated model development, and data assimilation are therefore central to improving predictions.
